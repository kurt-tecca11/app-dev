# My Favorite Movie: Hereditary (2018)

## Introduction

**Hereditary** is a 2018 psychological horror movie directed by *Ari Aster*. It follows a family who experiences disturbing and mysterious events after the death of their grandmother.

## Why I Like This Movie

I like this movie because of its *unique storyline*, disturbing atmosphere, and unexpected scenes.

Some things I like about it are:

- Great horror story
- Unexpected plot twists
- Amazing acting
- Interesting characters
